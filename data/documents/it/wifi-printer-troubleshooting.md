# Office Wi-Fi and Printer Troubleshooting

## Office Wi-Fi networks
- **NovaCorp**: company laptops, connects automatically with the device certificate. No password is needed.
- **NovaGuest**: visitors and personal devices only, internet access only, daily access code at reception.
Company laptops must never use NovaGuest to reach internal systems.

## Company laptop cannot connect to NovaCorp
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on, then select NovaCorp again.
2. Check that the date and time on the laptop are correct: a wrong clock makes the device certificate invalid.
3. Restart the laptop to renew the certificate and network settings.
4. Try another floor or meeting room to rule out a faulty access point.
5. If other colleagues nearby cannot connect either, report it as a possible network outage (P2 if a whole team is blocked).
6. If only your laptop fails, open a P3 ticket with the time of the problem, your location and a screenshot of the error.

Do not connect a company laptop to a personal hotspot to bypass the problem without IT approval.

## Printing
Printers are managed through **NovaPrint** follow-me printing: send the job to the "NovaPrint-Secure" queue, then badge in at any office printer to release it.

If a print job does not appear at the printer:
1. Check that you sent it to NovaPrint-Secure, not to a specific printer name.
2. Confirm you are connected to NovaCorp or the VPN; printing does not work from NovaGuest.
3. Jobs expire after 24 hours; send the document again.
4. If your badge is not recognised, contact the Service Desk to re-link it (P4 request).

Paper jams and empty toner can be reported with the QR code on the printer. Do not open the fuser area of a printer.
