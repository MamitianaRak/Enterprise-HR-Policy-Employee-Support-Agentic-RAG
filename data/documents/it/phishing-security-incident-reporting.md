# Phishing and Security Incident Reporting

## Recognising phishing
Be suspicious of any message that creates urgency, asks you to sign in through a link, requests payment or gift cards, or comes from an unexpected sender. NovaRetail IT never asks for your password or MFA code by email, chat or phone.

## If you receive a suspicious email
1. Do not click links, open attachments or reply.
2. Use the **Report Phishing** button in Outlook. It sends the message to the Security team and removes it from your inbox.
3. If the button is not available, forward the email as an attachment to security@novaretail.example and then delete it.

## If you clicked a link or entered your credentials
This is treated as a **P1 security incident**. Act immediately:
1. Disconnect from the VPN and Wi-Fi, but do not shut down the laptop: Security may need its memory for investigation.
2. Change your password from another trusted device using the self-service identity portal.
3. Call the IT Service Desk hotline and say "possible account compromise". Do not wait for a ticket response.
4. Tell the Service Desk which link you opened, what you entered, and at what time.
5. Do not try to clean the laptop yourself or run tools downloaded from the internet.

## If you opened an attachment
Disconnect from the network, leave the laptop powered on, and call the Service Desk hotline. Endpoint security will isolate the device remotely if needed.

## What happens next
Security reviews sign-in activity, revokes active sessions, and resets MFA if required. You may be asked to re-enrol your authenticator app. Reporting quickly is always the right decision: no employee is penalised for reporting a mistake.
